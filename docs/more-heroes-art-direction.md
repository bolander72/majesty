# More Heroes: retirement and future art direction

Mike requested retirement on October 29, 2026 after playing the release and
finding the repeated stock imagery unsuitable. Adventurers Guild and The Royal
Menagerie remain active. More Heroes is not to be reactivated until requested.
Its source and saved actor definitions remain available for compatibility.

## Quality standard approved October 30, 2026

Mike approved the textured Dark Paladin chapterhouse recreation after the
11:23 AM comparison as the visual direction for its replacement and all future
guilds and heroes. This supersedes the earlier smooth live building as the
world-art benchmark. Apply this aesthetic to every new guild or hero, including
content outside More Heroes. Each addition should
feel like a polished, believable part of Majesty's world. Quality and internal
consistency take priority over the number of guilds released.

Take pride in the complete player experience. Attractive, carefully finished
imagery is a release requirement across the building, hero, guild-panel scene
and menus. Functional integration alone is not sufficient. Review each
addition in-game beside stock content and revise weak artwork before release.

- Give each guild and hero a distinct identity through strong original artwork,
  readable silhouettes, equipment, portraits and animations. Carry that identity
  consistently through the world sprites, recruitment UI, selection cards,
  framed emblems and ability presentation.
- Build believable medieval fantasy architecture that suits the guild's purpose,
  culture and heroes. Use convincing materials, proportions and construction.
  Avoid futuristic forms, crude geometric stand-ins and deliberately blocky
  artwork. Review clarity and finish at actual game scale.
- Give the guild panel a composed, attractive painted scene with a readable focal
  subject, following the revised Dark Paladin interior's standard. Preserve
  Majesty's native framing, palette, typography and interaction states.
- Keep appearance, lore, names, equipment, voice and combat sounds, abilities
  and building function coherent. Choose audio that fits the character; do not
  carry over conflicting speech or combat voices from a borrowed class.
- Finish the intended progression and controls. Where the design includes
  upgrades, make them work and give each tier appropriate artwork and benefits.
  Include matching construction, damage and destruction states. Expose only
  commands supported by the chosen native controller, and test every visible
  button. Reusing a stock panel layout does not establish valid command routes.
- Introduce guilds gradually, completing and reviewing one coherent guild and
  hero package at a time. Verify recruitment, combat, progression, native UI
  and save/reload compatibility before release. Apply the existing explicit
  authorization requirement for enabling retired content.

Use this benchmark to develop each guild's own character and architecture;
the roster should offer a variety of equally polished, cohesive designs.

## Native world-art correction — October 30, 2026

Mike's 11:23 AM town screenshot revealed that the released Dark Paladin world
building does not meet the native Majesty aesthetic. Its pale flat walls,
oversized blocks, broad uniform roof surfaces and clean geometric edges read
as a toy model beside the marketplace, palace and zoo. The earlier release
is not an accepted visual benchmark for future world-building art. Working
upgrades and distinct silhouettes do not establish stylistic acceptance.

Use those neighboring native buildings as the material, detail, lighting and
pixel-scale reference. Require fine irregular masonry and visible mortar,
individually readable weathered roof tiles, recessed openings, convincing
wood and iron details, deep contact shadows and grounded placement. Match
Majesty's richly textured sprite finish and muted material palette. Retain
readable silhouettes without smooth low-detail CAD or plastic-looking forms.
Review all completed tiers side by side with stock buildings in the same town
at normal game scale before calling the visual work finished.

A replacement level-three visual study is saved in the workspace at
`output/more-heroes/dark-paladin/v5-style-study/majesty-style-study.png`.
It is a generated in-context art-direction study, not an installed sprite,
proof of unchanged screenshot pixels, or a completed native resource package.
The next production artwork must carry this textured treatment through the
cumulative level-one, level-two and level-three designs and their matching
construction, damage and destruction states. Existing tier labels, upgrade
behavior and save compatibility remain required.

## Visual acceptance

A new class needs a distinct silhouette, costume, equipment and animation,
a legible portrait and selection image, recognisable ability icons, and an
individual guild emblem. Tinting a stock hero or renaming a stock portrait does
not meet this requirement. New art should fit Majesty's painterly fantasy world,
palette, lighting and native interface proportions.

Begin with one complete hero before committing to all thirteen. Review the
walking, attack, casting, hit and death states in every direction required by
the actual native actor resource. Check attachment points, selection bounds,
foot placement, shadows and animation timing at game scale. Concept art is not
a finished sprite or a tested game asset.

Review the hero beside stock heroes in a busy town and in combat. Test the
portrait, selection card, recruitment button and ability icons at native size.
Palace building-list emblems must use the existing opaque 25-by-25 framed
contract. Preserve the native bevels, typography, selection and disabled states.

## Native tier naming and upgrade behavior

Mike clarified these requirements on October 30 after reviewing the live guild:

- Follow stock Blacksmith and Wizards Guild conventions in building menus:
  show the base building name for the first tier, then distinct `Level 2`,
  `Level 3` and later labels wherever those tiers are listed. Multiple tiers
  must never appear as indistinguishable repeated names. Keep names readable
  inside the stock row width and retain the full guild identity on its panel.
- An upgrade starts from the completed previous tier. Preserve the standing
  building while workers add to it; reserve bare foundations and initial
  building frames for new construction. Check both 1-to-2 and 2-to-3 work.
- Each completed tier needs substantial, clearly visible new architecture at
  normal game scale. Add coherent wings, towers or fortifications to the
  existing structure. Level 2 must visibly extend level 1; level 3 must retain
  the level-1 and level-2 architecture and visibly extend that combined building.
  Continue this cumulative progression for any later tiers. Give every tier
  its own completed artwork. Small decorative changes alone do not establish
  a tier.
- Review initial construction, active upgrades, completed tiers and restored
  saves against equivalent stock buildings. Verify the selected checkpoint
  actually contains the tier being reviewed. Preserve placement, framing,
  selection feedback and the features built in earlier tiers.

The Domain presentation pass follows the frozen original-art producer and
owns the final tier labels and upgrade-frame bindings. Future asset rebuilds
must include this pass before qualification or publication.

## Guild visibility, names, scaffolding and scale — October 30, 2026

Mike clarified that normal Dark Paladins must read as solid characters with
visible legs and feet, including newly recruited heroes. Validate opaque
interior pixels through the native renderer, readable shin/boot contrast and
cloth hems that leave the legs visible. Preserve native spell-controlled
appearance separately from class-default cosmetic effects.

Use the full names `Dark Paladin's Guild`, `Dark Paladin's Guild Level 2` and
`Dark Paladin's Guild Level 3` in native building lists and tier labels. Keep
legacy internal actor identifiers stable for existing saves and callbacks.

Both upgrade paths require visible native-style timber scaffolding: posts,
platforms, diagonal braces and ladders around the additions, while the earlier
building remains standing. Completed tiers must expand the visible footprint
with coherent connected architecture. Compare actual dimensions with stock
guilds and verify placement and selection, rather than accepting equal tiny
footprints at every tier.

### Original animation and readable architecture

Mike explicitly required custom world animations on October 30, 2026. Every
new guild and hero must ship its own coherent animation artwork, including
ambient activity, cloth, attached torch flames, damage and collapse. Borrowed
stock effect sprites are not acceptable, even when native controller metadata
is reused. Review animation in motion at native scale; a still render cannot
qualify attachment, scale, occlusion or timing.

Dark Paladin level 3 replaces the oversized front gate with an open courtyard,
low walls, a rear watchtower and an upper training wing. The hall stays visible.
The refined hero uses slimmer fitted armor, smaller shoulders and shield, solid
rounded boots and less exaggerated stride. Preserve all directions and states.

The Dark Paladin animation implementation includes twenty-frame primary guild
state artwork and original working/damage effect channels. Native controllers
choose when a state advances; do not add a separate idle timer or assume that
an idle standing building continuously plays every frame. Review active native
playback and attachment after changing animation records; retaining a stock
record layout alone does not prove that a controller plays its effect layers.
