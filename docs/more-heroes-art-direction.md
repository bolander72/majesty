# More Heroes: retirement and future art direction

Mike requested retirement on October 29, 2026 after playing the release and
finding the repeated stock imagery unsuitable. Adventurers Guild and The Royal
Menagerie remain active. More Heroes is not to be reactivated until requested.
Its source and saved actor definitions remain available for compatibility.

## Visual acceptance

A new class needs a distinct silhouette, costume, equipment and animation,
a legible portrait and selection image, recognisable ability icons, and an
individual guild emblem. Tinting a stock hero or renaming a stock portrait does
not meet this requirement. New art should fit Majesty's painterly fantasy world,
palette, lighting and native interface proportions.

Begin with one complete hero before committing to all thirteen. Review the
walking, attack, casting, hit and death states in every direction required by
the actual native actor resource. Check attachment points, selection bounds,
foot placement, shadows and animation timing at game scale. Concept art is not
a finished sprite or a tested game asset.

Review the hero beside stock heroes in a busy town and in combat. Test the
portrait, selection card, recruitment button and ability icons at native size.
Palace building-list emblems must use the existing opaque 25-by-25 framed
contract. Preserve the native bevels, typography, selection and disabled states.

## Initial identity proposals

These are design options, not approved replacement art or new gameplay promises.
The final roster can be smaller if several classes do not have a clear identity.

| Current class | Distinct visual direction to explore |
| --- | --- |
| Necromancer | Bone reliquary carried on the back; hooked staff and layered burial cloth. |
| Fire Witch | Broad ember mantle, furnace lantern and a sweeping flame-casting pose. |
| Dark Paladin | Closed angular helm, asymmetric tower shield and heavy deliberate movement. |
| Cultist Healer | Root-woven medicine harness, ceramic vessels and hand-drawn ward symbols. |
| Wild Monk | Low unarmed stance, broad wrapped forearms and reed-and-leaf travel gear. |
| Lightning Lord | Forked conductor staff, high storm collar and sharp directional casting. |
| Dragoncaller | Scale-trimmed cloak, carved calling horn and a distinctive falconer's arm guard. |
| Assassin | Short split cape, compact paired weapons and a narrow crouched silhouette. |
| Fallen Angel | Clearly readable folded wings, broken halo and a long weighted weapon. |
| Minstrel | Shoulder-carried instrument, travelling performance costume and expressive poses. |
| Shapeshifter | Antlered hide mantle; coherent original human and transformed silhouettes. |
| Mystic | Floating ritual rings, layered geometric robes and measured gestures. |
| Timewalker | Offset clockwork harness, pendulum staff and deliberately staggered motion. |

For the first prototype, compare silhouette sketches and a portrait together,
then produce a complete animation sheet and native icon set. Integrate that
prototype in an isolated build, inspect the native UI, and verify selection,
combat and save/reload before considering public recruitment.
