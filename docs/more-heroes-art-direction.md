# More Heroes: retirement and future art direction

Mike requested retirement on October 29, 2026 after playing the release and
finding the repeated stock imagery unsuitable. Adventurers Guild and The Royal
Menagerie remain active. More Heroes is not to be reactivated until requested.
Its source and saved actor definitions remain available for compatibility.

## Quality standard approved October 30, 2026

Mike established the revised Dark Paladin preview as the quality benchmark
for gradually introducing the other More Heroes guilds. Each addition should
feel like a polished, believable part of Majesty's world. Quality and internal
consistency take priority over the number of guilds released.

- Give each guild and hero a distinct identity through strong original artwork,
  readable silhouettes, equipment, portraits and animations. Carry that identity
  consistently through the world sprites, recruitment UI, selection cards,
  framed emblems and ability presentation.
- Build believable medieval fantasy architecture that suits the guild's purpose,
  culture and heroes. Use convincing materials, proportions and construction.
  Avoid futuristic forms, crude geometric stand-ins and deliberately blocky
  artwork. Review clarity and finish at actual game scale.
- Give the guild panel a composed, attractive painted scene with a readable focal
  subject, following the revised Dark Paladin interior's standard. Preserve
  Majesty's native framing, palette, typography and interaction states.
- Keep appearance, lore, names, equipment, voice and combat sounds, abilities
  and building function coherent. Choose audio that fits the character; do not
  carry over conflicting speech or combat voices from a borrowed class.
- Finish the intended progression and controls. Where the design includes
  upgrades, make them work and give each tier appropriate artwork and benefits.
  Include matching construction, damage and destruction states.
- Introduce guilds gradually, completing and reviewing one coherent guild and
  hero package at a time. Verify recruitment, combat, progression, native UI
  and save/reload compatibility before release. Apply the existing explicit
  authorization requirement for enabling retired content.

Use this benchmark to develop each guild's own character and architecture;
the roster should offer a variety of equally polished, cohesive designs.

## Visual acceptance

A new class needs a distinct silhouette, costume, equipment and animation,
a legible portrait and selection image, recognisable ability icons, and an
individual guild emblem. Tinting a stock hero or renaming a stock portrait does
not meet this requirement. New art should fit Majesty's painterly fantasy world,
palette, lighting and native interface proportions.

Begin with one complete hero before committing to all thirteen. Review the
walking, attack, casting, hit and death states in every direction required by
the actual native actor resource. Check attachment points, selection bounds,
foot placement, shadows and animation timing at game scale. Concept art is not
a finished sprite or a tested game asset.

Review the hero beside stock heroes in a busy town and in combat. Test the
portrait, selection card, recruitment button and ability icons at native size.
Palace building-list emblems must use the existing opaque 25-by-25 framed
contract. Preserve the native bevels, typography, selection and disabled states.

## Native tier naming and upgrade behavior

Mike clarified these requirements on October 30 after reviewing the live guild:

- Identify every building tier clearly in native lists, using `Level 2`, `Level 3`, and so on for upgrades, as stock buildings do.
  Keep the base name consistent with the stock level-one convention. Keep names readable
  inside the stock row width and retain the full guild identity on its panel.
- An upgrade starts from the completed previous tier. Preserve the standing
  building while workers add to it; reserve bare foundations and initial
  building frames for new construction. Check both 1-to-2 and 2-to-3 work.
- Each completed tier needs substantial, clearly visible new architecture at
  normal game scale. Add coherent wings, towers or fortifications to the
  existing structure. Small decorative changes alone do not establish a tier.
- Review initial construction, active upgrades, completed tiers and restored
  saves against equivalent stock buildings. Verify the selected checkpoint
  actually contains the tier being reviewed. Preserve placement, framing,
  selection feedback and the features built in earlier tiers.

The Domain presentation pass follows the frozen original-art producer and
owns the final tier labels and upgrade-frame bindings. Future asset rebuilds
must include this pass before qualification or publication.

## Initial identity proposals

These are design options, not approved replacement art or new gameplay promises.
The final roster can be smaller if several classes do not have a clear identity.

| Current class | Distinct visual direction to explore |
| --- | --- |
| Necromancer | Bone reliquary carried on the back; hooked staff and layered burial cloth. |
| Fire Witch | Broad ember mantle, furnace lantern and a sweeping flame-casting pose. |
| Dark Paladin | Closed angular helm, asymmetric tower shield and heavy deliberate movement. |
| Cultist Healer | Root-woven medicine harness, ceramic vessels and hand-drawn ward symbols. |
| Wild Monk | Low unarmed stance, broad wrapped forearms and reed-and-leaf travel gear. |
| Lightning Lord | Forked conductor staff, high storm collar and sharp directional casting. |
| Dragoncaller | Scale-trimmed cloak, carved calling horn and a distinctive falconer's arm guard. |
| Assassin | Short split cape, compact paired weapons and a narrow crouched silhouette. |
| Fallen Angel | Clearly readable folded wings, broken halo and a long weighted weapon. |
| Minstrel | Shoulder-carried instrument, travelling performance costume and expressive poses. |
| Shapeshifter | Antlered hide mantle; coherent original human and transformed silhouettes. |
| Mystic | Floating ritual rings, layered geometric robes and measured gestures. |
| Timewalker | Offset clockwork harness, pendulum staff and deliberately staggered motion. |

For the first prototype, compare silhouette sketches and a portrait together,
then produce a complete animation sheet and native icon set. Integrate that
prototype in an isolated build, inspect the native UI, and verify selection,
combat and save/reload before considering public recruitment.
