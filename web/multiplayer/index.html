Keep the expedition together

Both players must be present to start or travel. If a partner disconnects, the room ends; the shared campaign cannot continue with their kingdom offline.

To continue an expanding world later, the host chooses Save and pause, then Close game and prepare resume. Prepare the resume file, open the game, and choose the named save through Load Game. Send the resume invitation to your partner. Both players must rejoin before continuing; later unsaved moves are not included.

Your previous campaign setup stays in the links, but each new chapter starts new kingdoms. Choose a new world name before moving to another chapter.

Co-op chapters share victory and defeat between allied kingdoms. Competitive play uses the original lobby's map and victory settings. After joining, the guest chooses Ready and the host chooses Start Game.
