Majesty launcher

Your kingdoms · Your machine

Keep your progress with you

One player backup includes all native save profiles, co-op chapter completion, and saved Frontier Atlas regions and camera positions on this browser. No game account is required.

Game files and mod assets are imported separately. A restored kingdom still needs the same game content. Multiplayer worlds keep their existing rules for joining and resuming; a backup does not let an absent partner's kingdom continue offline.

Save a player backup

Download a ZIP to your machine. You can keep that same file in Google Drive or another folder yourself.

Native files are read in small chunks. Backups support up to 3 GiB of save data and 16 MiB of Atlas metadata per world.

One completed archive stays temporarily on this browser. After your download finishes, clear that temporary copy to free space. Clearing it while downloading can interrupt the download.

Restore a backup

Choose a complete player backup to check its contents and integrity before restoring.

Restore requires an empty native save collection and no conflicting campaign or Atlas databases. Existing progress is preserved. Use the main launcher's Import saves for older native-only ZIPs.

Close the game and world tabs, then choose export or restore.

If a restore is interrupted, game startup is blocked until its incomplete new save collection is discarded. Keep your original backup file.
