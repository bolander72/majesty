The Frontier Pact

An experimental four-chapter cooperative campaign using Majesty's original kingdoms, heroes, economy and mission scripts. Both players choose the same chapter before creating or joining a multiplayer room. Two allied kingdoms share victory; losing either kingdom ends the mission for both.

In the native lobby, choose your starting forces and terrain. The chapter controls the actual objective shown in the campaign panel. Use 30,000 starting gold for the treasury and construction chapters. Wardens of the Pact requires a Warriors Guild and Temple to Dauros in each kingdom. Fortified Palaces starts with Warriors Guilds; the temples must still be built.

Saves and chapter completion stay on this browser. No game login is required.
