# Original artwork prompts

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool. These are source atlases for the
native asset pipeline, not published gameplay qualification.

## Trail Lodge

Transparent 2×2 atlas of one original medieval fantasy waystation, using a
fixed orthographic isometric camera, warm top-left lighting and hand-painted
classic PC strategy artwork. An octagonal stone building with a weathered
slate roof, an amber lantern beacon on its central tower, muted teal trail
pennants, an open map-table porch, travel supplies and stone trail markers.
Keep its footprint and scale consistent across four cells: half-built with
scaffolding, completed, active with brighter beacon, and a destroyed ruin.
No text, logos, grid, scenery or baked background; true alpha transparency.

## Wayfinder

Transparent 8×8 animation atlas of one original isometric hero. Tan travel
coat over a muted teal tunic, copper compass emblem, rolled bedroll, dark
boots, a low-brim hat and a sturdy polearm carrying an amber lantern. No hood,
bow, arrows or quiver. Hand-painted strategy-game sprites with consistent
scale, baseline, camera and warm top-left lighting, readable when small.
Columns turn through east, southeast, south, southwest, west, northwest,
north and northeast. Rows show standing, alternating walking steps, attack
wind-up, thrust, lantern casting, hit recovery and death. No text, grid or
scenery; true transparency. Review note: the generated recovery row is a
falling pose, so it is used only for dying and never for ordinary hit recovery.
